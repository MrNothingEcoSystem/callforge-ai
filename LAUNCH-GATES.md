# Launch Gates

CALLFORGE must not be described as launched or production-ready unless every applicable gate has independently accepted evidence.

## Current blocked gates

- M01 pull-request review and hosted CI.
- Production OAuth, RBAC, and tenant isolation.
- Durable workflow, outbox, reconciliation, backup, and restore.
- Trusted UAE compliance evidence and separate human approval.
- Authorized carrier and sandbox-to-production provider verification.
- Voice quality and safety benchmarks.
- Console, integrations, remote MCP, and sandbox billing.
- Independent security/reliability review.
- Legal/privacy review, deployment approval, DNS, monitoring, rollback, and support readiness.

Engineering completion does not grant permission for live telephone calls, customer billing, production deployment, public plugin publication, protected-branch merge, or real customer data processing.
